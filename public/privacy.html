Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Resonance is a music player that turns your music into visuals. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't track you.

Your music

Your music never leaves your device. Files you open, and sound from your microphone or other apps, are analysed on your device while you listen. They aren't uploaded or kept.

Kept on your device

  • Your settings, and the folder you last opened
  • The name you give the app, if you give one
  • How often you've visited, and how many times you've played each artist
  • Where you left off in a song
  • Lyrics that have been looked up, so they aren't looked up twice

None of this is sent anywhere. To clear your name, visits and play counts, open the Visuals menu and choose Forget me.

Lyrics

Lyrics are off until you turn them on. When they're on, the title and artist of each song you play are sent to LRCLIB, a free lyrics service, to find its words. Your music itself is never sent. LRCLIB will see your IP address, as any website does.

What's playing on your PC

When you use System audio, the Windows app reads the title, artist and artwork that Windows reports for what's playing. This stays on your device unless lyrics are turned on.

Updates and hosting

The app downloaded from the website checks GitHub for new versions, and the Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store. The website is hosted on Vercel. These services see your IP address, as any website does, and nothing about your music.

Children

Resonance doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age.
